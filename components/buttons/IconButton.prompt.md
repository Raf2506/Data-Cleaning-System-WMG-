The 40px circular chrome control — back arrow, carousel paddle, wishlist heart, share, hide-filters toggle.

```jsx
<IconButton name="heart" label="Add to favourites" />
<IconButton name="chevron-left" label="Previous" variant="onImage" />
```

Always circular (`--radius-full`) at 40px; hit area is padded to 48px by surrounding layout. Never colored — ink on light, canvas on ink.
