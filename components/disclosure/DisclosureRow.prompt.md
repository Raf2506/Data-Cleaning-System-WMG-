Accordion row used for PDP information sections and membership FAQs.

```jsx
<DisclosureRow label="Shipping & Returns">Free standard shipping on orders over $50.</DisclosureRow>
<DisclosureRow label="How do I join?" variant="faq" />
```

24px vertical padding, chevron right-aligned, 1px hairline divider below each row. `faq` swaps the label to the display-tuned heading cut.
